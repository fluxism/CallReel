<b>CallReel</b> records your phone calls, and the video of your video calls, on a non-rooted phone. It is free and open source, has no ads and collects nothing.

<p><b>This app is a fork.</b> It is a modified version of another open source call recorder. The original project and its author are credited, with a link, in the app (Settings, About) and in the README of the source repository. CallReel is not the original app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by its author.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul><li><b>Video call recording</b> (Android 14+): when a recorded call is a video call, the screen is recorded with the call audio into an MP4 file next to the audio file<ul><li>Starts by itself for video calls, or from a button on the notification</li><li>Works with apps that use Android Telecom, such as WhatsApp</li><li>Off by default</li></ul></li><li>Records <b>both sides of phone calls</b> (incoming and outgoing), also with Bluetooth or a headset</li><li><b>Separate uplink and downlink audio</b> inside a stereo recording</li><li><b>Security</b> toggles that start Shizuku only when it is needed</li><li><b>Automatic call recording</b> with exclusion rules: anonymous calls, specific contacts, all contacts</li><li>Several codecs: <b>Opus</b>, <b>AAC</b>, <b>HE-AAC-v1</b> and more</li><li>Runs only when a call happens, with no persistent background process</li></ul>

<p><b>Requirements</b></p>
<ul><li><b>Android 11</b> or newer for audio. <b>Android 14</b> or newer for video and for third-party apps</li><li><a href="https://github.com/thedjchi/Shizuku">Shizuku</a></li></ul>

<p><b>Good to know</b></p>
<ul><li>Video recording records the whole screen, exactly as you see it, including other apps you open during the call</li><li>The app uses hidden Android APIs through Shizuku. It can break with a new Android release or on some devices</li><li>Setup instructions are in the <a href="https://github.com/fluxism/CallReel/blob/main/docs/SUPPORT.md">documentation</a></li></ul>
